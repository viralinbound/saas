06 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

Clear answers about pricing, GST invoicing, domains, and store setup.

How does the 2% fee on sales work? ▼
The 2% sales fee applies only to completed orders processed on your store (ex GST). It is billed monthly with transparent reporting.
Is custom domain & SSL included for free? ▼
Yes! Every SuperShowroom plan includes a free custom domain (.com or .in), SSL certificate, and automated HTTPS security.
How long does it take to launch my online store? ▼
Our standard setup takes 3 to 7 business days from catalog handover. We handle design, build, and delivery partner integration.
Can I use my existing GST and bank account? ▼
Yes! We configure your automated GST invoices, razorpay/UPI payment gateways, and direct payout bank accounts during onboarding.
